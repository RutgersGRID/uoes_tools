What goes in each blank?
Condition
The circumstances under which learners will demonstrate their learning. The condition describes the performance, not the instruction. This could be something they are given, like a map, something they must do without, like a calculator, or the setting or timing, like the end of a semester.
Audience
Who the learners are. Examples include students, third-grade students, or new hires.
Behavior
What observable, measurable action will learners perform, and what content, skill, or product will they act on or produce? Examples include verbs from Bloom's Taxonomy such as label, calculate, assemble, etc. Avoid verbs you cannot measure or check, like understand or appreciate.
Degree (optional)
How well the learners must perform, if applicable. Some possible examples could be an expectation of accuracy, completeness, time, or quality. If there are any required elements, such as 'include three supporting sources', or if the objective must align with a rubric, such as 'meet all criteria on the rubric', include that as part of the degree.

Bloom's Taxonomy Verbs

Action verbs for each tier of Bloom's Taxonomy, listed from the highest tier down. These are the verbs Newton, Da Silva and Peters (2020) found the most agreement on: each one appears in more than half of the 47 published verb lists they reviewed, and is consistently placed at the same tier.

Evaluation
Rate, evaluate, assess, judge, justify
Synthesis
Create, compose, argue, design, plan, support, revise, formulate
Analysis
Analyze, question, differentiate, experiment, examine, test, categorize, distinguish, calculate, contrast, outline, infer, discriminate, compare
Application
Operate, apply, use, demonstrate, solve, produce, prepare, choose
Comprehension
Translate, paraphrase, discuss, report, locate, generalize, explain, classify, summarize
Knowledge
List, define, recall, state, label, repeat, name

The paper's authors also suggest avoiding: appreciate, know, familiar, aware, understand, select, explain, relate, arrange, choose. A few of these appear in the tiers above as well. The authors flag them because different institutions place the same verb at different tiers, which is exactly the confusion a learning objective is meant to avoid.

Newton, P. M., Da Silva, A., & Peters, L. G. (2020). A pragmatic master list of action verbs for Bloom's Taxonomy. Frontiers in Education, 5, 107.
