UOES Applications for Faculty

  • Learning Objective Builder

    Build well-formed learning objectives by filling in the blanks: audience, behavior, condition, and degree.

  • Course Content Planner

    Organize your course content with backwards design: set goals, plan assessments, prioritize topics, and map everything onto a module-by-module schedule.

  • Course Workload Estimator

    Estimate how many hours per week your course asks of students, based on reading, writing, videos, discussions, exams, assignments, and class meetings — split into independent and contact time.

  • Credit Hour Planner

    Turn a course’s credit hours into an hours-per-week budget for online, traditional, or blended formats — then check whether the activities planned for a module actually fit inside it.

  • Bloom’s Verbs

    Explore the different levels of Bloom's Taxonomy and how to use them in your teaching.
