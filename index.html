UOES Applications for Faculty

Welcome! These tools are designed to help faculty build out their courses — from writing clear learning objectives to planning content, estimating student workload, and checking credit hours. Pick a tool below to get started.

Looking to learn more about course design?

Visit the UOES Resource Library for information on course design foundations, active learning, creating course media, and more.

For additional support, you can also review UOES' course design process or schedule a course design consultation.

Our Tools

  • Learning Objective Builder

    Build well-formed learning objectives by filling in the blanks: audience, behavior, condition, and degree.

  • Course Map Builder

    Organize your course content with backwards design: set objectives, plan assessments, prioritize assignments, and map everything onto a module-by-module schedule.

  • Learning Activity Estimator

    Estimate how long a learning activity will take students so you can plan each week or module with real numbers.

  • Credit Hour Planner

    Turn a course’s credit hours into a time-based budget for online, traditional, or blended formats — then review whether the activities planned for the course actually fit inside it.

  • Bloom’s Verbs

    Explore the different levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy and how to use them in your teaching.
